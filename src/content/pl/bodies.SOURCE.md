# Status of the texts in `bodies.json`

The body card content (EMI-218) is placeholder text written by the planner until the content team delivers the final texts. Numbers in the texts follow the NASA Planetary Fact Sheet (<https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/>). The JSON schema is closed (ADR-010), so the status of each entry lives in this table, not in the JSON.

`status` is `placeholder` or `approved`. Replacing a text with the delivered one changes its row to `approved`.
